Behaviour

The upload limit goes up to {{.MaxUploadCeiling}} MB, which is room for a stacked astrophotography frame or a scan. A limit still exists because without one a single request can fill the disk, and running out of space fails far more messily than a refused upload. If you sit behind a reverse proxy, check its own body size limit too: nginx defaults to 1 MB and will refuse a large upload before Broadside ever sees it.

Password length is the property that actually resists an attack on a stolen hash, which is why it is the only password rule here. It cannot go below {{.MinPasswordFloor}}.
